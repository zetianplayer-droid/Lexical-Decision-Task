Lexical Decision Task

You will see a series of letter strings, one at a time.

If the letters make up a real word, press the RIGHT arrow key.
If they do not make up a word, press the LEFT arrow key.

On a phone or tablet: tap the WORD button (right) if the letters make a real word, or the NOT A WORD button (left) if they do not.

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can.

The attempt number is assigned automatically and cannot be changed.
